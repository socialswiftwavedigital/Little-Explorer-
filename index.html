✦✦ 8 Play Zones, One Visit ✦✦

From ball pits to climbing walls, kinetic sand to Lego. Every zone is different and kids love all of them.

Slides & Climb

Kids race down bright slides and conquer soft climbing structures. Burns energy fast, builds balance and kids always want another go.

Ball Pit

Jump into thousands of soft, clean coloured balls for pure unfiltered fun. Sanitized daily and fully supervised.

Climbing Wall

Colourful handholds at different heights for toddlers and older kids. Kids test their limits and feel genuinely proud.

Pretend Play

A kid-sized kitchen, market and salon where children act out real-life roles. Great for language, social skills and imagination.

Sensory Room

A quiet, low-light space for children who need a break from busy play. Especially great for younger toddlers.

Lego Table

Thousands of colourful LEGO bricks and zero screens. Kids focus, build and problem-solve for hours.

Kinetic Sand

Mess-free sand that moulds, squishes and sculpts. Kids cannot put it down. Builds fine motor skills.

Latcher Table

Bolts, latches, locks and doors to open and figure out. A Montessori-style activity wall that keeps toddlers absorbed.
