✦✦ What's Inside Our Play Centre ✦✦

Each zone is different. Some get kids moving, some keep them calm, all of them keep them happy.

Slides & Climb

Kids race down bright slides and conquer soft climbing structures. Burns energy fast, builds balance.

Ball Pit

Jump into thousands of soft, clean coloured balls. Sanitized daily and fully supervised.

Climbing Wall

Colourful handholds at different heights for toddlers and older kids. Build grip and confidence.

Pretend Play

A kid-sized kitchen, market and salon. Great for language, social skills and imagination.

Sensory Room

A quiet, low-light space. Especially great for younger toddlers and neurodiverse kids.

Lego Table

Thousands of colourful LEGO bricks and zero screens. Kids focus and problem-solve for hours.

Kinetic Sand

Mess-free sand that moulds, squishes and sculpts. Builds fine motor skills.

Latcher Table

Bolts, latches, locks and doors to open. A Montessori-style activity wall that keeps toddlers absorbed.
