7 Best Kids Birthday Party Places in Islamabad (Honest 2026 Review)

September 2026 7 min read Islamabad, Pakistan

Planning a kids birthday party in Islamabad involves more decisions than it used to. The number of venues has grown, pricing varies widely and what is "included" in a package differs enormously between places. This is an honest, practical guide for parents.

What to Look for in a Kids Birthday Venue

Before comparing venues, decide what matters most to you:

  • Age of the birthday child — a 2-year-old's party needs different things than an 8-year-old's
  • Guest count — how many kids are coming? Venues have different capacity limits
  • Budget — packages in Islamabad range from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 60,000+
  • What is included — décor, cake, host, food, games
  • Location — travelling with young children is tiring; closer is better

2. Funville — Centaurus Mall

Larger space, more rides. Better for older kids (7+). Packages typically start around Rs. 20,000 but the "included" items are fewer and the add-ons are expensive.

3. Home Parties with Activity Packages

Some activity companies in Islamabad offer to come to your home with a craft or cooking activity for groups of 10–20 kids. Typically Rs. 8,000–15,000. Good if you want a small, intimate party.

4. ARY Mundo — Bahria Town

More of an entertainment centre than a play zone. Birthday packages are available but the space is better for older children and the per-head cost is higher.

5. Hotel Banquet Halls — Islamabad

Marquees and banquet halls can be hired for larger parties (30–80 kids). Pricing varies widely. You bring your own entertainment and catering. Higher logistics burden.

6. Outdoor Venue Hire — Farm Houses

Popular for summer parties in the evening. Completely dependent on weather and requires significant logistics. Not recommended for children under 5.

7. Cafe and Restaurant Private Rooms

Some cafes in F-6, F-7 and F-8 offer private dining room hire for birthday parties. Good for intimate parties of 8–12 children. Limited play activity.
